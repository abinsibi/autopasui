# How to Play Battleship

## Game Overview

Battleship is a classic naval combat strategy game where players try to sink each other's fleet of ships by guessing their locations on a grid. This digital version offers both single-player (against computer) and multiplayer (against another player) modes.

## Game Setup

### Starting the Game

1. When you first open the game, you'll see a splash screen with two options:
   - **Single Player**: Play against the computer
   - **Multiplayer**: Play against another player online

2. Select your preferred game mode by clicking on the corresponding button.

## Ship Placement Phase

### Your Fleet

You have 5 ships of different sizes:
- **Carrier**: 5 squares long
- **Battleship**: 4 squares long
- **Cruiser**: 3 squares long
- **Submarine**: 3 squares long
- **Destroyer**: 2 squares long

### Placing Your Ships

1. **Drag and Drop**: Click and drag each ship from the display area to your grid (the left grid).
2. **Rotate Ships**: Click the "Rotate Your Ships" button to toggle between horizontal and vertical orientation before placing a ship.
3. **Placement Rules**:
   - Ships cannot overlap with each other
   - Ships cannot extend beyond the grid boundaries
   - Ships cannot be placed diagonally

4. Once all your ships are placed, click the "Start Game" button to begin playing.

## Gameplay

### Taking Turns

1. **Your Turn**: When it's your turn, the display will show "Your Go"
   - Click on a square in the opponent's grid (right grid) to fire a shot
   - If you hit an enemy ship, the square will be marked
   - If you miss, the square will be marked differently

2. **Computer's Turn** (in single-player mode):
   - The computer will automatically take its turn after you
   - The display will show "Computer's Go" during this time
   - Wait for the computer to complete its move

### Hit and Miss Indicators

- **Hit**: When you successfully hit a ship, the square will turn red with a visual indicator
- **Miss**: When you miss, the square will be marked with a splash indicator

### Winning the Game

The game continues until one player has sunk all of the opponent's ships. A ship is sunk when all of its squares have been hit.

The first player to sink all of the opponent's ships wins the game!

## Multiplayer Mode

In multiplayer mode, the gameplay is similar, but you'll be playing against another human player instead of the computer:

1. Wait for another player to join the game
2. Place your ships as described above
3. Take turns firing at each other's grids
4. The first player to sink all of the opponent's ships wins

## Game Controls

- **Start Game Button**: Begins the game after ship placement
- **Rotate Button**: Toggles ship orientation during placement phase
- **Grid Clicks**: Fire shots at opponent's grid during gameplay

## Tips and Strategies

1. **Strategic Ship Placement**:
   - Avoid placing ships in predictable patterns
   - Consider placing some ships along the edges of the grid
   - Spread your ships out to make them harder to find

2. **Efficient Targeting**:
   - When you get a hit, try targeting adjacent squares to find the rest of the ship
   - Use a checkerboard pattern for initial shots to maximize efficiency
   - Pay attention to the sizes of ships that haven't been sunk yet

3. **Ship Tracking**:
   - Keep track of which enemy ships you've already sunk
   - Remember that ships can only be placed horizontally or vertically, not diagonally

Enjoy playing Battleship!
